## Intake and targets

Every request receives a private case ID. Ask for the URL or record ID, exact field, proposed correction, public supporting source, requester's relationship to the subject, and whether any evidence must remain private.

- Confirmed malicious destinations: suppress immediately; investigate after containment.
- Potentially material factual error: triage within one working day.
- Ordinary request: acknowledge within two working days.
- Decision or progress update: within seven calendar days.
- Urgent rights withdrawal: disable the affected display/export first when the claim is credible, then determine what audit trace may lawfully remain.

These are internal targets until support hours and absence cover are assigned.

## Decision rules

Private evidence may inform verification but is never published without permission. A seller may submit a factual response; it cannot veto independent evidence or reveal a complainant. Editors record the old value, decision, public sources, affected pages/exports, reviewer, and time. Contested claims use precise attribution instead of an unsupported verdict.

Every accepted correction or withdrawal must propagate to rendered pages, downloadable JSON, generated metadata, cached/CDN copies, analytics dimensions, search indexes, and future snapshots. A rights withdrawal removes content and history where required; only a permitted minimal audit trace remains.

## Appeal

An appeal must be decided by someone other than the original editor. If no independent reviewer is available, the contested claim stays withheld or attributed as unresolved. Commercial status never changes access to correction or appeal.
