# Discovery and pilot runbook

This file makes the research executable without pretending it has happened.

## Discovery sample

Recruit distinct people making current decisions: at least three active Equihash miners, two prospective hardware buyers, two pool/operator or infrastructure participants, and three people who use adjacent comparison tools but do not already know Equihash.com. Record relationship and conflicts; do not count the owner or implementation team as comprehension participants.

Observe a real decision using the participant’s present tools before showing the prototype. Capture the decision, alternatives opened, evidence checked, uncertainties, action, and whether a later outcome can be sampled. Never collect wallet addresses, credentials, worker names, or unnecessary personal data.

## Counterbalanced task test

Use one complete task, such as “Given this machine, country, electricity cost and pool, explain the change in expected income and what must be checked.” Half the participants use their present workflow first; half use Equihash.com first. Then switch. A neutral facilitator records time, wrong conclusions, missing-cost recognition, evidence opening, confidence calibration and whether the output changed an action.

The broader pilot gate requires at least 8 of 10 distinct participants to identify:

1. which values are source claims versus calculations,
2. the Wcash affiliation where relevant,
3. missing costs and uncertainty,
4. current versus historical availability.

## Six-week operator pilot

For six complete weeks, record refresh success, stale duration, correction acknowledgement/decision time, source withdrawal handling, false-current incidents, rollbacks, unanswered support periods and attributable repeat use. Do not merge partial weeks into a success claim.

## Distribution and paid-service experiments

Run one attributable distribution experiment at a time with a fixed channel, audience, start/end, cost ceiling and target task. A scoped paid pilot needs a written deliverable and actual payment; interest or a friendly promise is not revenue evidence. Procurement, reporting and integration pilots must disclose conflicts and never alter editorial order.
