## `equihash-industrial-hero-v1.webp`

Original illustrative hero image generated for equihash.com on 7 October 2026 with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, then resized to 1920 pixels wide and encoded as WebP at quality 84.

The prompt requested a wide, high-end architectural visualization of anonymous industrial ASIC mining infrastructure, with graphite steel surfaces, restrained amber status lights, negative space for HTML copy, and no text, logos, coin symbols, people or watermarks. It is an illustration and does not depict a facility owned or operated by equihash.com.

The original generated PNG remains in the owner's Codex generated-image library. This WebP is the project asset used by the homepage.
