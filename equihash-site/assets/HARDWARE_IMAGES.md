- Antminer Z15: BITMAIN Support, [How to set up Z15 Server](https://support.bitmain.com/hc/en-us/articles/7942533055641-How-to-set-up-Z15-Server), attachment `mceclip0.png`.
- Antminer Z11: BITMAIN Support, [ANTMINER Z11](https://support.bitmain.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019882054-ANTMINER-Z11), attachment `Z11__.jpg`.
- Antminer Z9: BITMAIN Support, [ANTMINER Z9](https://support.bitmain.com/hc/en-us/articles/360006928613-ANTMINER-Z9), attachment `mceclip0.png`.
- Antminer Z9 Mini: BITMAIN Support, [ANTMINER Z9 mini](https://support.bitmain.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003878794-ANTMINER-Z9-mini), attachment `mceclip0.png`.
- Innosilicon A9++ ZMaster: Innosilicon's [A9++ product page](https://innosilicon.shop/html/a9%20%20-miner/index.html), image `a9pp_big_01.jpg`.

The five manufacturer source files were fetched and converted to WebP on 7 October 2026. No generative imagery is used.
